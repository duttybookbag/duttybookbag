Dutty Ashy Win_G Foundation Cares About You

Life is hard. It doesn't matter where you live, what language you speak, or what your situation is — everybody goes through tough times. Bills pile up. Stress takes over. You feel stuck. We've been there. We understand.

The Dutty Ashy Win_G Foundation believes that no one should have to face it alone. This page exists because we care — not as a business move, not for attention, but because helping people get back on their feet is the whole reason this Foundation exists.

We care about your mental health. We care about your family eating tonight. We care about your lights staying on. We care about you finding a path forward — no matter how small the first step is.

Every link on this page is free, verified, and here for you. No sign-ups, no ads, no catch. Just real help from people who genuinely want to see you win.

// Break Free From Debt

💳 Credit Card Relief & Debt Help

Drowning in credit card debt? There are real programs — not scams — that can negotiate your debt down, set up payment plans, or even forgive portions of what you owe. Never pay someone upfront for debt relief.

⚠ Avoid Scams: Legitimate debt relief organizations will NEVER ask you to pay upfront. If someone says "pay us $500 and we'll erase your debt" — it's a scam. The resources below are verified nonprofits and government programs.

USA

NFCC — Free Credit Counseling

1-800-388-2227

National Foundation for Credit Counseling. Free or low-cost sessions with certified counselors. They negotiate with your credit card companies directly.

nfcc.org →
USA

Debt Management Plans (DMP)

Through NFCC counselors — consolidate all credit cards into one lower monthly payment. Interest rates reduced. No loans needed.

nfcc.org/debt-management →
USA

Credit Card Hardship Programs

Call your credit card company directly and say "I'm experiencing financial hardship." Most have internal programs that lower your rate, waive fees, or pause payments for 3-12 months. You just have to ask.

USA

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

File complaints against credit card companies, learn your rights, get free debt advice from the government.

consumerfinance.gov →
UK

StepChange — Free Debt Advice

0800 138 1111 (Free)

UK's leading debt charity. Free debt management plans, IVA advice, bankruptcy guidance.

stepchange.org →
UK

National Debtline

0808 808 4000 (Free)

Free debt advice for England, Scotland, Wales. Phone, webchat, online tools.

nationaldebtline.org →
