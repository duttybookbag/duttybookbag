// What It Is

DBB Alpha Oscillator

Who's Winning — Bulls or Bears?

DBB Alpha Oscillator measures the dominance battle between bulls and bears across 7 timeframes simultaneously (1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h, 1D) and displays the result as a single oscillator line and histogram.

It uses a dual-engine system — Engine 1 analyzes volume power and Engine 2 analyzes momentum strength. You can run them individually or combined for maximum conviction. Higher timeframes carry more weight in the final score.

The result is a percentage from -100% (full bear dominance) to +100% (full bull dominance). Zero means neither side is winning. The further from zero, the stronger the dominance.

It does not tell you what to do. You read the dominance and make your own decisions.

// What's Inside

Systems & Features

Dual Engine System
// ENG 1 (Volume) · ENG 2 (Momentum) · ENG 1+2 (Combined)

Two independent analysis engines that measure market dominance differently. Engine 1 focuses on volume power — how much volume is behind each side. Engine 2 focuses on momentum strength — how aggressively price is moving in each direction. Run them separately to isolate signals, or combine them for the strongest conviction reads.

7-Timeframe Analysis
// 1m · 5m · 15m · 30m · 1h · 4h · 1D

Scans all 7 timeframes simultaneously and weighs them — higher timeframes carry more influence than lower ones. This means the daily and 4-hour readings impact the final score more than the 1-minute, giving you a balanced view that isn't dominated by short-term noise.

Dominance Percentage
// -100% to +100% scale

The final output is a single percentage. Positive = bulls are winning. Negative = bears are winning. Zero = neutral. The further from zero, the stronger the dominance. Displayed as both a line and a histogram so you can see direction and magnitude at a glance.

Power Level Labels
// MINIMAL · LOW · MEDIUM · HIGH · MAXIMUM

A live label on your chart shows the current dominance direction (🟢 BULL or 🔴 BEAR), the percentage, the power level, and which engine mode is active. Quick visual reference without checking the oscillator line.

Crossover Detection
// Configurable threshold levels

Set custom threshold levels and the oscillator will mark when dominance crosses above or below them. Default crossover at ±16% with additional thresholds at ±20%, ±40%, and ±60%. Zero-line crossovers are tracked separately — these mark the shift from bull to bear or vice versa.

Background Zones
// Visual dominance zones

Optional colored background zones that shade the chart when dominance exceeds your crossover threshold. Green zone = bull dominance territory. Red zone = bear dominance territory. Makes it easy to see at a glance which side is in control.

Smoothing Option
// Optional SMA smoothing

Apply smoothing to the oscillator output to reduce noise. Adjustable length from 1 to 10 periods. Use it to see the trend of dominance rather than bar-by-bar fluctuations. Toggle on or off — your choice.

Full Alert System
// Crossover · Zero-cross · Threshold · Extreme

Built-in alerts for every key event — crossover above/below threshold, zero-line crosses (bull/bear shift), threshold breaks at each level, and extreme dominance above 75%. Set up TradingView alerts so you never miss a shift in dominance.

Timeframe Controls
// Include or exclude specific timeframes per engine

Choose which timeframes feed into each engine. Want Engine 1 to skip the 1-minute? Turn it off. Want Engine 2 to only use higher timeframes? Disable the lower ones. Full control over what data goes into the final dominance score.
