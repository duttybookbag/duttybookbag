// Common Questions

Still Not Sure?

Do I need to pay for TradingView?
No. A free TradingView account is all you need. You do NOT need their paid plans. The free Basic account gives you full access to invite-only indicators like ours.
Is my credit card safe on Gumroad?
Yes. Gumroad uses Stripe and PayPal to process payments — the same systems used by Amazon, Uber, and Shopify. Your card details are encrypted and never stored on Gumroad's servers. You can also pay with PayPal for an extra layer of protection.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, anytime. There are no contracts and no cancellation fees. You can cancel directly from your Gumroad account or through the link in any of your receipt emails.
Why do I need to send my TradingView username?
TradingView's invite-only system works by username. I manually add each person by their exact username. If I don't have it, TradingView has no way to give you access. That's why it's so important to get it right.
What if I don't see the indicator after being added?
Try refreshing your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R), clearing your cache, or logging out and back in. On TradingView, go to Indicators → Invite-Only Scripts. If it's still not there, double-check your username matches exactly what's on your TradingView profile.
I don't trust putting my card online. What else can I do?
You can pay with Bitcoin (BTC) or Ripple (XRP) instead. After sending the crypto equivalent of $4.99, email [email protected] with your TradingView username and transaction ID.
Is subscribing on YouTube the same as paying on Gumroad?
No. YouTube and Gumroad are completely separate. Following or subscribing on YouTube gives you access to videos only. To get the indicators, you must pay on Gumroad. They are two different things.
