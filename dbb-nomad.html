DBB NOMAD v5.0

A Complete Chart Overlay System

DBB NOMAD v5.0 is a complete chart overlay system combining bull/bear signal detection, moving averages (EMA 8, EMA 21, SMA 50, SMA 64, SMA 200), price average, CT1, multi-timeframe analysis, volume power, directional intensity, and compression detection — all in one indicator on your chart.

// What's Inside

Systems & Features

Bull / Bear Signal System
// Triangle markers on your chart

Detects bullish and bearish conditions based on multiple confluence factors. When conditions align, a green triangle (bull) or red triangle (bear) appears on your chart. Background color changes to match the active signal direction.

Moving Average Suite
// EMA 8 · EMA 21 · SMA 50 · SMA 64 · SMA 200

Five moving averages with fully customizable periods, colors, line width, and opacity. Toggle each one individually. Use them together or pick the ones that fit your strategy. Each can be enabled or disabled from the Performance Masters panel.

Price Average System
// Volume Weighted Average Price

Live Price Average plotted directly on your chart. See where the volume-weighted price sits relative to current price action. Fully customizable color and toggle.

CT1
// Upper · Middle · Lower with fill

Standard CT1 with adjustable colors for upper, middle, and lower bands. Includes a fill between the bands with adjustable opacity so you can see the squeeze and expansion zones visually.

Multi-Timeframe System
// X1 · X2 · X3 · X4 · X5 · X6

Monitors conditions across multiple timeframes and triggers signals when key thresholds are hit. Shows which score level triggered directly on your chart. A proprietary detection system built for speed and accuracy.

Xpush System
// Cross-timeframe signal scoring

When a bull or bear signal fires, the Xpush system watches for confirmation across your selected score levels — X1 through X5. When a score level confirms, its label appears on the chart at the exact bar it triggered. Each score level can be enabled or disabled individually and shares the same toggle as the Multi-Timeframe system.

✔ X1 = fastest · X2 · X3 · X4 · X5 = higher timeframe confirmation
Compression Detection
// Squeeze and expansion states

Detects when price is being squeezed into a tight range (compression) or when it has expanded out. Compression often precedes big moves. Visible across multiple timeframes so you can spot when several are coiling at once.

Volume Power Analysis
// Volume direction and strength

Measures volume activity relative to the 20-period average and determines whether the volume is supporting bullish or bearish price action. Shows whether volume is strong or weak and which direction it favors.

Directional Intensity (DI)
// Dominant momentum tracking

Shows the dominant directional momentum on each timeframe. Once a direction is confirmed, it holds — giving you a clear read on whether buyers or sellers are in control. Designed to filter out noise and show the bigger picture.

Performance Masters Panel
// Toggle everything on or off

Every single system can be individually enabled or disabled. Running on a slow device? Turn off the multi-timeframe system. Want a clean chart? Hit the Clean Chart toggle and everything disappears. You control exactly what runs.
