// What It Is

DBB Wave Stages

Market Momentum & Wave Tracking

DBB Wave Stages is an oscillator-based indicator built for scalpers and short-term traders. It tracks market momentum, detects wave stages, measures compression levels, and monitors multi-timeframe volume power — all displayed in a compact live table on your chart.

It tells you where the market is in its current wave cycle (B1, B2, B3 for bull waves — R1, R2, R3 for bear waves), how strong the drive behind the move is, and whether momentum across multiple timeframes is aligned or conflicting.

It does not tell you what to do. You read the data and make your own decisions.

// What's Inside

Systems & Features

Wave Stage Detection
// B1–B3 Bull · R1–R3 Bear

Tracks the progression of directional waves. When a bullish wave begins, it counts stages — B1, B2, B3. Same for bearish waves — R1, R2, R3. Higher stages indicate the move has been going on longer. A warning flashes yellow when the wave reaches an advanced stage, signaling potential exhaustion.

Market Bias Tracking
// BULL · BEAR · NONE

Determines the current market bias based on signal conditions. Shows BULL, BEAR, or NONE in the table. Bias resets when the opposite signal fires. All wave stages and drive logic follow the current bias direction.

Pulse Compression System
// Low · Mid · High · Fired

A proprietary compression detection system with three levels — low compression, mid compression, and high compression. When compression fires (released), momentum is breaking out. Displayed as colored dots or a line on your chart.

Drive Momentum System
// 6-state momentum engine

A proprietary system that combines multi-timeframe volume power with wave stage data to determine the current drive state. Six possible states: Flat (gray), Yellow/Warning (caution), Green (bullish), Red (bearish), Bright Green (strong bullish with advanced wave), Bright Red (strong bearish with advanced wave). Plotted as a colored line on your chart.

Power System (4 Timeframes)
// Short-term focused · up to 1 hour

Monitors volume and price direction across four short-term timeframes up to the 1-hour. Built for scalpers and short-term traders who need fast reads. Each power column shows whether that timeframe has active bullish or bearish volume. When multiple timeframes agree, the drive system responds accordingly. Each power slot can be toggled individually.

Momentum Oscillator
// Normalized momentum histogram

A custom momentum oscillator that measures price momentum combined with volume strength. Color-coded: positive increasing (accelerating up), positive decreasing (slowing up), negative increasing (accelerating down), negative decreasing (slowing down). Plotted as a histogram.

T/F Agreement System
// True / False oscillator alignment

Checks whether the drive plot and momentum oscillator agree on direction. Shows 🔥 (True — aligned) or ❌ (False — conflicting). When all systems point the same way, conviction is higher. Multiple comparison modes available.

AMP Volume
// Volume as percentage of average

Shows current volume as a percentage of the 20-period average. 100% means volume matches the average. Above 150% indicates elevated activity. Below 50% indicates quiet conditions. Displayed in the table for quick reference.

Compact & Full Table Modes
// Your choice of display

Full mode shows every system column — T/F, Drive, Power 1–4, Force, AMP, Momentum, Stage, and Bias. Compact mode strips it down to just T/F, Stage, Bias, and AMP for a minimal footprint. Fully customizable size, position, and emoji vs text display.
