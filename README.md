## 🖥️ How to Add the Indicator to Your Chart

Once you've been granted access:

```
Log In to TradingView → Open Any Chart → Click "Indicators" → "Invite-Only Scripts" Tab → Click & Add to Chart
```

### Detailed Instructions

**Step 1 — Log In to TradingView**
Go to [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com) and sign in.

**Step 2 — Open a Chart**
Click the **Chart** option from the top navigation bar. Any chart works — stocks, crypto, forex, etc.

**Step 3 — Click the "Indicators" Button**
At the top of the chart screen, click the **Indicators** button (bar chart icon). You can also press the `/` key as a shortcut.

**Step 4 — Go to the "Invite-Only Scripts" Tab**
In the left sidebar of the Indicators menu, look for the tab labeled **Invite-Only Scripts**. Click it — your indicators will appear in the list.

**Step 5 — Add to Your Chart**
Click the indicator name and it will instantly load onto your chart. Customize settings by clicking the ⚙️ gear icon.

> ✅ The indicators will always be available in your "Invite-Only Scripts" tab as long as your access is active.

## ❓ Common Questions

**I don't see the "Invite-Only Scripts" tab at all**
This tab only appears after you've been granted access. Make sure your correct TradingView username has been submitted. The tab will appear automatically once access is granted.

**Do I need a paid TradingView plan?**
No! A free TradingView account is enough. The paid plan requirement is only for the person *publishing* the script, not for users accessing it.

**I was added but still don't see it — what do I do?**
Try refreshing your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R), clearing your cache, or logging out and back in. If it still doesn't appear, double-check that the username you provided matches exactly what's shown on your TradingView profile.

**Can I see the source code?**
No — and that's by design. Invite-only scripts protect the source code. You can use the indicators fully on your charts, but the underlying Pine Script code remains hidden.

**Can I use it on mobile / TradingView app?**
Yes! Once added, your account has access across all TradingView platforms including the mobile app.

**What's the difference between all the indicators?**
The **DBB Analytics Table** is the main multi-timeframe data table. **duttyb@g@von blocks** overlays order blocks and price average on your chart. **DBB NOMAD v5.0** is a full chart overlay with signals, moving averages, and multi-timeframe detection. **DBB Wave Stages** is an oscillator for scalpers tracking wave progression and drive momentum. **DBB Alpha Oscillator** measures bull/bear dominance across 7 timeframes in a sub-pane. The **Intensity Monitor** is a free bonus measuring momentum across 5 systems.
