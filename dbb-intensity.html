// Overview

What Does It Do?

The DBB Intensity Monitor measures the intensity of what the market is doing right now — across five different lenses. It shows a small live table directly on your TradingView chart.

Each column tells you a different story about momentum, volume, and candle behavior. The goal is to give you more information at a glance — not to tell you what to do. All decisions are yours. The indicator only measures. You interpret.

// The Five Systems

Each Column Explained

RSI Velocity
// Column label: RSI V

Measures how fast the RSI is moving — not where it is, but how quickly it's changing bar to bar. Fast movement means momentum is accelerating.

  • HIGH — RSI moved 3+ points
  • MID — RSI moved 1–3 points
  • LOW — RSI barely moved
Volume ROC
// Column label: Vol

Tracks volume activity and measures whether it is significant or not. When volume is meaningful it often signals something important is happening in the market.

  • HIGH — volume changed 100%+
  • MID — volume changed 50–100%
  • LOW — volume is quiet
Candle Strength
// Column label: Candle

Compares the current candle body size to the recent average. A large body means price moved decisively in one direction — strong conviction.

  • HIGH — body is 1.5× average
  • MID — body is near average
  • LOW — small or weak candle
Pure RSI
// Column label: Pure RSI

Simple and clean. One question: is RSI above or below 50? This gives you the big-picture RSI position without any complexity.

  • UP — RSI is above 50
  • DOWN — RSI is below 50
Pure Volume
// Column label: Pure Vol

Measures whether volume activity is significant, and which way price moved during that activity. Identifies whether volume is supporting the current price move or not.

  • UP — high volume + price up
  • DOWN — high volume + price down
  • LOW — volume is not significant

// Reading the Table

How to Read It

🟢

Multiple Columns Green

When RSI V, Vol, and Candle all show bright green together — multiple systems are detecting high-intensity upward movement at the same time. This alignment is worth paying attention to.

🔴

Multiple Columns Red

Same idea in the other direction. Multiple columns red simultaneously means high-intensity downward movement across different measurements at once.

⚪

Everything Gray

Low intensity across the board. The market is quiet or consolidating. No strong signals present. Many traders use this as a signal to step back and wait.

🔀

Mixed Colors

Some columns up, some down. The market is sending conflicting signals — possible indecision or a transition. This is normal. Use your own judgment.

// Common Question

RSI V vs. Pure RSI

RSI Velocity answers: "How FAST is RSI moving right now?" — It's about speed and acceleration. A big jump in RSI (even from 60 to 64) reads as HIGH intensity.

Pure RSI answers: "WHERE is RSI sitting overall?" — Simply above or below 50. The big-picture position, not the speed.

You can have a HIGH RSI Velocity while Pure RSI is still DOWN (below 50). You can also have a LOW RSI Velocity while Pure RSI is UP. Both columns together give you the full picture — speed and position.

// Getting Started

Quick Tips

Start with the colors. You don't need to read the numbers at first. Green = intensity up. Red = intensity down. Gray = quiet. That's it.
Look for agreement. The more columns that agree, the stronger the signal. One red column is noise. Three red columns at once is something else.
Gray is useful too. A LOW reading isn't bad — it tells you the market is resting. Sometimes the best move is to wait.
Use the timeframe setting. Set RSI to 1h or 1d for swing trading, or keep it at the current timeframe for scalping.
