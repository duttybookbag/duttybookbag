// What It Is

Long Long Short Short

Cross Detection & Structure Analysis

Long Long Short Short (LLSS) is a chart overlay indicator that detects golden crosses, death crosses, and pullback signals — while also mapping price structure using a multi-pivot zigzag system and similar price pattern detection.

It includes a live info table showing ticker, price, price average, volume, market sentiment, 52-week range, and more — everything you need at a glance without cluttering your chart with separate indicators.

Built with a volatility filter so signals only fire when conditions are right — not during low-volatility sideways chop.

It does not tell you what to do. You see the crosses, the structure, and the data — then make your own decisions.

// What's Inside

Systems & Features

Golden & Death Cross Detection
// SMA 50 × SMA 200 crossovers

Detects when the SMA 50 crosses above the SMA 200 (Golden Cross — bullish) or below it (Death Cross — bearish). These are classic long-term directional signals. Marked with green and red triangles on your chart with optional background color changes.

Pullback Detection System
// Post-cross pullback confirmation

After a golden or death cross fires, the system watches for a pullback and then confirms when momentum resumes in the original direction. Displayed as diamond shapes on your chart. Helps you catch re-entries after the initial cross signal.

Multi Zag Structure
// Pivot-based price structure mapping

Maps the swing highs and swing lows of price action using a configurable pivot system. Draws lines connecting the major turning points so you can see the structure of the market — higher highs, lower lows, and everything in between. Adjustable length for sensitivity.

Similar Price Pattern Detection
// Finds tops and bottoms at similar price levels

Automatically scans recent pivot highs and pivot lows and highlights when price returns to a similar level. Potential double tops and double bottoms are color-coded — red labels for similar highs, green labels for similar lows. Configurable similarity tolerance percentage.

Moving Average Suite
// SMA 8 · SMA 21 · SMA 50 · SMA 200

Four simple moving averages with fully customizable colors. Toggle each one individually. SMA 50 and SMA 200 are on by default for cross detection. SMA 8 and SMA 21 available for shorter-term analysis.

Price Average + Delta Tracking
// Price relative to volume-weighted average

Optional price average line on your chart plus a delta calculation showing exactly how far price is from price average — in both dollar amount and percentage. Displayed in the info table for quick reference.

Volatility Filter
// Filters signals during low-volatility chop

Uses an internal compression detection method to determine if the market is in a low-volatility squeeze. When enabled, cross signals are suppressed during sideways conditions so you only get signals when the market is actually moving.

Live Info Table
// All your key data in one place

A compact table on your chart showing ticker, current price with percentage change, price average, price average delta, high/low, volume vs average, market sentiment, 52-week range, and RSI. Every row can be toggled on or off. Fully customizable position and background opacity.

Pattern Analysis Lines
// Connect the dots on price structure

Optional dotted lines connecting recent pivot highs and pivot lows separately, showing the trend of swing points over time. Choose how many points to connect and customize the color, width, and transparency of the lines.

Full Alert System
// Golden cross · Death cross · Price × SMA crosses

Built-in alert conditions for golden cross, death cross, and price crossing any of the four SMAs. Enable only the alerts you care about. Set up TradingView alerts so you never miss a signal.
