💡 Quick Tips To Free Up Money

📺 Subscriptions: You're spending /month on subscriptions. Cancel what you don't use daily. Even cutting one $15 service saves $180/year.

🍔 Eating Out: You're spending /month on takeout/restaurants. Cooking at home is 3-5x cheaper. Try meal prepping on Sundays.
