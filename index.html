What You Get for $4.99

📊

7 Timeframes — One Table

1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h, and 1D all visible simultaneously. Stop switching timeframes manually — it's all there at once.

📈

I/O — RSI Across All Timeframes

See whether RSI is above or below 50 on every timeframe in one glance. Green = above 50. Red = below 50. The exact value shown.

🔒

S — Compression Detection

Detects when price is squeezed (🔒) or expanded (⚡) using an internal compression system. Know when a big move might be building.

⬆️

SR — Trend Direction + Dominance

Trend Direction direction on higher timeframes. Combined dominance score on 1m and 5m. All in one column.

💧

PWR — Volume Power

Measures volume activity and direction on each candle. Green = strong volume up candle. Red = strong volume down candle. Gray = no conviction.

🔵

DI — Directional Intensity

Shows the dominant directional momentum on each timeframe. Once a direction is confirmed, it holds — giving you a clear read on whether buyers or sellers are in control. Designed to filter out noise and show the bigger picture.

🌊

BIA Row — Market Bias + Wave Stages

Confirms when the market has entered a directional wave (BULL or BEAR) and tracks how far the wave has progressed — B1, B2, B3 or R1, R2, R3. Flashes yellow at warning stage.

📏

H/L Row — 252-Bar High / Low

Shows the historical high and low for context. Know instantly whether price is near an extreme or in the middle of its range.
