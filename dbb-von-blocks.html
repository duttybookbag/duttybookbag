// Overview

What Does It Do?

duttyb@g@von blocks is a multi-system overlay indicator that puts order blocks, VWAP levels, and confluence zones directly on your TradingView chart. It runs across 7 timeframes simultaneously and scores each zone by how many key levels are nearby.

Instead of manually drawing support and resistance zones, the indicator finds them for you — identifies high-volume price zones, plots them visually, and marks them as mitigated when price returns and closes through them. VWAP bands give you the broader price context around every zone.

It does not tell you what to do. You see the zones and make your own decisions.

// The Six Systems

Everything Inside It

VWAP System
// Volume Weighted Average Price

Plots the daily VWAP with two standard deviation bands above and below. Gives you the fair value price for the day and shows how far price has stretched from it.

  • Orange dashed line = VWAP center (daily fair value)
  • Blue lines ×1 = first standard deviation band
  • Blue lines ×2 = second standard deviation band (extreme)
  • Lines extend past and future — you control how far
Order Blocks
// Current Timeframe Zones

Detects high-volume pivot zones on your current chart timeframe. These are areas where significant buying or selling occurred — price often returns to them.

  • Green box = bullish order block — high-volume up candle at a swing low
  • Red box = bearish order block — high-volume down candle at a swing high
  • Volume must exceed the threshold you set to qualify
MTF Order Blocks
// Multi-Timeframe Zones

Simultaneously pulls order blocks from all 7 timeframes — 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h, and 1D — and overlays them on your current chart. Higher timeframe zones carry more weight.

  • Dashed green = bullish MTF order block
  • Dashed purple = bearish MTF order block
  • Each zone labeled with its source timeframe
  • Toggle individual timeframes on/off in settings
Confluence Detection
// Zone Strength Scoring

Scores each order block by how many VWAP levels are within a set distance. The higher the score, the more key levels are stacked at that zone — potentially more significant.

  • Score 1 = one VWAP level nearby — basic zone
  • Score 2 = two VWAP levels nearby — stronger zone
  • Score 3+ = three or more levels — high confluence zone
  • Set minimum score to filter weak zones out
Mitigation Shapes
// When a Zone Gets Closed

When price returns to an order block and closes through it, the zone is "mitigated" — it's been filled. A small shape appears on the chart marking exactly where and when this happened.

  • Shape appears at the bar where mitigation occurs
  • Choose from circle, diamond, cross, triangle, square, or flag
  • Separate colors for bullish vs bearish mitigation
  • The mitigated zone is automatically removed from the chart
Alert System
// 10 Alert Conditions

Full alert system covering every event the indicator detects. Set them up in TradingView once and get notified on your phone, email, or webhook.

  • New bullish or bearish order block detected
  • Bullish or bearish mitigation event
  • High confluence zone (score 3+)
  • Price touching a VWAP level
  • MTF bullish or bearish alignment signal

// Reading the Chart

How to Use It

🟩

Green Box on Your Chart

A bullish order block — high-volume buying occurred here. Price often returns to these zones. The closer to a VWAP level, the higher its confluence score and the brighter its color.

🟥

Red Box on Your Chart

A bearish order block — high-volume selling occurred here. Same logic in the other direction. Watch for price reactions when it approaches these zones.

📐

Dashed Boxes

MTF order blocks — these come from a different timeframe than the one you're currently viewing. A dashed green box on your 5m chart might be a 1h bullish order block. Higher timeframe zones are generally considered more significant.

🟠

Orange Dashed Line — VWAP

The daily Volume Weighted Average Price. This is where most volume has traded for the day. Price above VWAP is generally considered stronger, below is weaker. The bands show how far price has stretched from fair value.

⚫

Small Shape on Chart — Mitigation

When you see a small circle, diamond, or other shape appear, it means an order block was just mitigated — price closed through it. The zone is removed and the shape marks the exact moment it happened.

🎯

Zone Color Changes — Confluence Level

The color of each zone's border changes based on its confluence score. Green border = score 1. Yellow/orange border = score 2. Bright lime/fuchsia border = score 3+. The brighter, the more VWAP levels are stacked at that zone.

// Getting the Most From It

Quick Tips

Start with confluence score 2 or higher. Setting the minimum confluence score to 2 filters out weaker zones and only shows you areas where an order block and at least two VWAP levels are stacked together.
MTF zones from higher timeframes carry more weight. A 1D order block sitting on your 5m chart is more significant than a 1m order block. Check the label on each box to see where it came from.
VWAP is a daily reset. The VWAP resets every day at market open. Early in the session it's more volatile — later in the day it becomes a stronger reference as more volume builds behind it.
Watch the mitigation shapes. When an order block gets mitigated it means price filled that zone. Mitigated zones often flip — a bullish block that gets mitigated can become resistance. The shape tells you exactly when this happened.
Use the clean chart toggle. The indicator has a master switch that turns everything off at once — useful when you need a clear view of price action without any overlays.
Set up the alerts. The indicator has 10 alert conditions. Set up the ones that matter to you — new order blocks, mitigations, high confluence — and let TradingView notify you instead of watching the chart all day.
