// How To Use

Guide

Quick Start
// Each tool works standalone or synced together
DCA IN
Enter each buy — price, quantity, fee. Hit Calculate DCA to see your average cost, total invested, and P&L. Hit Send to Portfolio to add it automatically.
SCALED EXIT
Enter your position size and average entry. Add price targets and what % you want to sell at each level. See exactly how much USD you collect at every step.
CONVERTER
Select a coin, enter either a coin amount or USD amount — the other fills in automatically. Price pulls from live data. You can override the price manually any time.
DIVIDENDS
Enter stock price, shares, and annual dividend per share. Toggle DRIP on to see how reinvesting compounds your shares year by year.
WHAT IF
Enter current price and how much you hold. See your value at 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x. Set a target price to see exactly what it's worth when you get there.
PORTFOLIO
Add any holding manually or use the Send to Portfolio buttons from DCA or What If. See your total value, gain/loss, ROI, fees, dividends, and pie charts all in one place.
⚡ DBB POSITION SIZING
Built on Edward Thorp's Kelly Criterion. Enter your capital, asset price, max risk %, entry price, and support/resistance levels. Set your conviction score 1–10. The calculator gives you 4 position sizes — FULL, 75, HALF, QUARTER — so you always know exactly how many shares or coins to buy based on your confidence in the trade.
// AUTO-FILL SYNC — HOW IT WORKS
When Sync is ON — type an asset name in any calculator and the same name fills across DCA, Scaled Exit, Converter, and Portfolio automatically. Same with price and quantity. You can still override any field manually at any time — just type your own value and it sticks.

Turn sync OFF using the toggle bar above if you want each calculator to work independently with different assets at the same time.
