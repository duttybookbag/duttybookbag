Guide

Quick Start
// Fill in any calculator — everything syncs together automatically
// HOW THE SYNC WORKS
All 8 calculators are connected. When Sync is ON, typing a price, asset name, budget, or quantity in any calculator pushes the same value to all matching empty fields everywhere else. Sync never overwrites a field you already typed in — it only fills empty ones.

Ticker shortcuts: Type any coin symbol (BTC, ETH, XRP...) into any name or price field — a dropdown appears with the live price. Tap it and every calculator fills at once.

🔄 Re-Sync button: If some fields are empty after filling others, hit Re-Sync in the top bar to push all current values to any remaining empty fields.

Toggle Off: Hit Toggle Off in the top bar to work each calculator with a different asset at the same time — no syncing.

Quantity: All calculators show a Quantity field. For planning tools (Money Management, Pyramid) it calculates from their own budget and price. For tracking tools (DCA, Scaled Exit, Dividend, What If, Portfolio, Converter) it syncs from your actual position size. Read-only everywhere except Converter.
DCA TRACKER
Track every buy you made for an asset.

1. Type your asset name (or ticker like XRP).
2. For each buy — enter the Price you paid, the Quantity you bought, and an optional Fee.
3. Hit + Add Another Entry for each additional buy.
4. Hit Calculate DCA to see your average cost, total spent (including fees), and how much you are up or down right now.

After calculating: Your total quantity and total spent push automatically to Scaled Exit, What If, Dividends, and Portfolio.

Current Price field: Optional — shows your real-time profit or loss. Type a price or a ticker symbol and the live price fills in.

Potential Exit Price: Optional — shows what your position would be worth if it hit that price.
MONEY MANAGEMENT
Tells you exactly how much to buy based on your budget and risk. Built on Edward Thorp's position sizing method.

1. Enter your Budget — total money you are working with.
2. Pick asset type (Stock or Crypto).
3. Enter the Asset Price and your Max Risk % — how much of your budget you are willing to lose on this trade.
4. See DBB FULL · 75 · HALF · QUARTER — four position sizes from aggressive to conservative.

5. Add Entry Price, Resistance (your target), and Support (your floor) to see:
— Hypothetical stop zone and target zone
— Risk/Reward ratio (good trades are 1:2 or better)

Conviction slider 1–10: Shows which tier Thorp recommends based on your confidence.

Quantity here = Budget ÷ Entry Price. It is a planning number, not synced from DCA.
PYRAMID
Plan a multi-level buy strategy — buy more as price drops.

1. Enter your Budget and Asset Name.
2. Add Price Levels — each one is a price where you plan to buy. Label them if you want (e.g. "strong support zone").
3. Pick a weight mode: Auto (bigger buy at lower prices), Equal (same amount at each level), or Aggressive (much heavier at bottom).
4. Hit Build Pyramid to see exactly how much to spend and how many coins to buy at each level.

Auto-sync to Money Management: As you type price levels, the highest automatically fills Resistance and the lowest fills Support in Money Management — live as you type.

Quantity here = Budget ÷ average of all your price levels. Planning number only.
SCALED EXIT
Plan how to sell your position in stages instead of all at once.

1. Enter your Asset Name, Quantity, and the price you bought at (Avg Entry Price).
2. Add price levels — each one is a price where you might sell. Enter the % of your position you would sell there and an optional note.
3. Hit Model Potential Exits to see: how much USD you get at each level, your profit or loss, and what you still hold after each sale.

Potential Best-Case Price: Optional — adds a hypothetical best-case return to the results.

After calculating: Name, quantity, and entry price push to Portfolio if those fields are empty.

Educational only — not a recommendation to sell.
DIVIDENDS
See how much income your holdings could pay you over time.

1. Enter Asset Price and Quantity you own.
2. Enter the Annual Dividend per asset (check your broker or stock info page for this number).
3. Pick frequency: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual.
4. Enter how many years to project.
5. Toggle DRIP On to reinvest every payment — your quantity grows each period and compounds over time.
6. Hit Calculate Dividends.

Potential Exit Price: Optional — stacks a hypothetical capital gain on top of your dividend total.
WHAT IF
See what your holdings would be worth at different prices.

1. Enter the Current Price and how much you hold (Quantity).
2. Optionally enter your Budget (total you spent) to see ROI %.
3. Hit Project It to see your value at 2×, 3×, 5×, and 10× from current price.

Potential Exit Price: Optional — shows the exact value and return at your specific target.

After calculating: Quantity and price push to Dividends and Portfolio if those fields are still empty.

Short Mode flips the projections to show -25%, -50%, -75%, -90% drops instead.
PORTFOLIO
Track all your holdings in one place.

1. Enter Asset Name, buy price, quantity, optional fee, current price, and optional annual dividend.
2. Hit Add to Portfolio. Repeat for each holding.

Or use the Send to Portfolio buttons from DCA, What If, or Scaled Exit — they fill everything automatically.

See: total invested, current value, overall profit/loss, ROI %, fees paid, estimated dividend income, 2×–10× projections, and two charts — holdings breakdown by value and invested vs gain.

Each entry clears the input fields so you can add the next holding right away.
CONVERTER
Convert any crypto to any world currency or to another crypto instantly.

1. Pick your coin from the From dropdown — the live price fills automatically.
2. Pick the currency or crypto you want to convert to.
3. Type either a coin amount or a currency amount — the other side calculates instantly.

Quantity field: Type a quantity here to sync it to all tracking calculators (Scaled Exit, Dividend, What If, Portfolio, DCA).

Global currencies: Covers every world currency across Americas, Caribbean, Europe, Africa, Middle East, Asia, and Oceania. Your local currency is auto-detected on load.

💱 Currency toggle: Hit 💱 Convert Results in the top bar to flip all result boxes across every calculator to your local currency at once.
// 📉 SHORT MODE
Hit 📉 Short Mode in the top bar to flip all calculators for short trading. Labels swap (Support becomes Stop Loss, Resistance becomes Cover Target), projections flip to price drops (-25%/-50%/-75%/-90%), and P&L math reverses. A red banner shows at the top so you always know Short Mode is active. Most traders are bulls — Short Mode is off by default.
