🔄 Auto-Fill Sync
// Smart calculator linking

The Auto-Fill Sync bar at the top of the page links all calculators together. When it is ON, typing the same asset name (like BTC or TSLA) in any calculator will automatically fill that asset's name, price, and quantity into all other calculators. This saves time when you are working with the same asset across multiple tools.

✅ Only syncs when the same asset name is detected
✅ Never overwrites something you typed manually
✅ Toggle it OFF anytime if you want each calculator independent
✅ Prices auto-pull from the live feed if the asset is in the ticker
📡 Market Watch — Live Prices
// 15 crypto + TSLA, SPY, QQQ

Shows live crypto prices from CoinGecko and delayed stock prices. Green percentage means the asset is up in the last 24 hours. Red means it is down. Prices refresh automatically every 2 minutes. Stock prices are delayed and are for reference only — do not use for live trading decisions. Crypto prices may also be slightly delayed.

📥 DCA In — Dollar Cost Average
// Track your buy entries

What it does: Calculates your average entry price when you buy an asset in multiple purchases over time instead of all at once.

1. Enter the asset name (BTC, TSLA, etc.)
2. Add each buy — price paid, quantity bought, and any fee
3. Click + Add Another Entry for each purchase
4. Enter the current price to see your profit or loss
5. Click Calculate DCA to see your blended average cost
6. Hit Send to Portfolio Tracker to add it automatically
💡 Tip: DCA works best for assets you believe in long term. Buying more when price drops lowers your average cost.
