// Overview

What Does It Do?

The DBB Clean Analytics Table is a multi-timeframe analysis system that puts a live data table directly on your TradingView chart. It reads across up to 7 timeframes simultaneously — 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h, and 1D — and shows you key market data for each one at a glance.

Instead of switching timeframes manually to check what the market is doing, everything is visible in one compact table. RSI position, momentum compression, SAR direction, volume power, directional intensity, market bias, and historical high/low — all live, all in one place.

It does not tell you what to do. You read the data and make your own decisions.

// The Six Columns

Every Column Explained

TF
// Timeframe

Shows which timeframe each row represents — 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h, or 1D. The background color also reflects the DI state so you know the directional status at a glance before reading anything else.

I/O
// RSI In / Out of Range

Shows the current RSI value for that timeframe. Color tells you direction instantly.

  • Green = RSI above 50 — momentum on the upside
  • Red = RSI below 50 — momentum on the downside
  • Number shown = exact RSI value
S
// Compression / Squeeze State

Tells you whether price is currently squeezed or expanded. Uses an internal compression detection system.

  • 🔒 = Compressed (IN) — market is coiling, potential move building
  • ⚡ = Expanded (OUT) — market is already moving, squeeze released
SR
// SAR + Combined Dominance

This column does different things depending on the timeframe row.

  • 1h / 4h / 1D rows — shows SAR direction arrow (⬆️ or ⬇️)
  • 1m row — shows combined dominance direction (🟢 or 🔴)
  • 5m row — shows dominance percentage number
  • 15m / 30m rows — intentionally blank
PWR
// Volume Power

Shows volume activity for each timeframe. Tells you whether there is power behind the current move or not.

  • Green = strong volume activity on an UP candle — power move up
  • Red = strong volume activity on a DOWN candle — power move down
  • Gray = low volume activity — no conviction behind the move
  • Number shows raw volume (e.g. 142K, 1.2M)
DI
// Directional Intensity

Measures the dominant directional momentum on each timeframe. Once a direction is confirmed, it holds — filtering out noise to show whether buyers or sellers are in control.

  • B (Blue) = strong directional UP state confirmed
  • G (Gray) = strong directional DOWN state confirmed
  • Blank = no directional state confirmed yet on that timeframe
// Special Rows

The Bottom Rows

📊

BIA — Market Bias Row

The bias row summarizes the overall market condition based on wave analysis. It uses an internal multi-factor system to determine if the market has entered a directional phase.

BULL = internal conditions met — strong upward wave detected
BEAR = internal conditions met — strong downward wave detected
Blank = No confirmed bias yet
⚠ The BIA cell flashes yellow when a warning stage is reached. This means the wave has progressed significantly — not a signal to act, just an alert to pay attention.
🔢

Wave Stages — B1, B2, B3 / R1, R2, R3

Once a bias is confirmed, the indicator tracks how far the wave has progressed using stages. These appear in the S column of the BIA row.

B1
Early bull wave — just confirmed
B2
Bull wave developing — watch closely
B3
Late bull wave — warning level
R1
Early bear wave — just confirmed
R2
Bear wave developing — watch closely
R3
Late bear wave — warning level
⚡

PWR % in the BIA Row — AMP

The PWR cell in the BIA row shows the current volume amplification level. This is called AMP — Amplification.

Above 100% = volume is amplified — shown in blue, strong activity behind the market
Below 100% = volume is quiet — shown in orange, low activity
📏

H/L — Historical High / Low Row

The bottom row shows the highest high and lowest low over the past 252 bars on your current timeframe. This gives you context — where is price relative to its recent range?

Left number = 252-bar HIGH     Right number = 252-bar LOW
Use this to understand if price is near extremes or in the middle of its range.
// Reading the Table

How to Use It

🟢

Multiple Timeframes Aligned Green

When I/O shows green across 1m, 5m, 15m, and 1h at the same time — RSI is above 50 on all those timeframes. This means momentum is aligned across the board, not just on one timeframe. The more aligned, the more significant.

🔒

S Column Shows 🔒 Compression

When the S column shows 🔒 on multiple timeframes, the market is coiling. Price is being squeezed between tight bands. This often precedes a larger move — but it doesn't tell you which direction. Watch the other columns for clues.

⬆️

SR Column Shows SAR Arrows

On the 1h, 4h, and 1D rows — an ⬆️ means price is above the Parabolic SAR on that timeframe (momentum up). A ⬇️ means price is below it (momentum down). When higher timeframes show ⬆️ and lower timeframes are also green on I/O, that's strong alignment.

⚠️

BIA Row Flashing Yellow

When the BIA cell flashes yellow with ⚠️, a warning stage has been reached on the current wave. This is not a signal — it is an alert that the wave has extended significantly. Use your own judgment on what this means for your setup.

🔵

DI Column Shows B Across Timeframes

When the DI column shows B (blue) across multiple timeframes, buyers are dominant across the board. This directional read holds until sellers take over — giving you a clear picture of who is in control.

// Getting the Most From It

Quick Tips

Start with the BIA row. It gives you the big picture first — is there a confirmed bias or not? Then look at the columns for detail.
Look for timeframe alignment on I/O. One green row is noise. Three or four green rows at the same time is alignment. The more aligned, the more the market agrees across timeframes.
S column compression = potential. When multiple timeframes show 🔒, something is building. It doesn't tell you direction — use the SR column and I/O for clues.
PWR column confirms conviction. A move with green PWR behind it has volume support. A move with gray PWR is happening on low volume — weaker.
DI holds its direction. Once a timeframe shows blue (B), it stays that way until sellers take control. Use it to understand the longer-term directional momentum of each timeframe.
Turn off what you don't need. Every column and row can be toggled off in settings. If you don't use DI — hide it. Keep the table clean for your trading style.
