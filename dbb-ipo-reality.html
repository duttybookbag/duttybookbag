What The Data Says
⚡ Bottom Line
✅
Waiting is statistically smarter. The first-day pop is mostly hype — institutional investors, media buzz, and retail FOMO. The price rarely reflects true fundamental value on Day 1.
📉
60% of IPOs underperform the S&P 500 over 3 years. You'd often do better buying an index fund and going to sleep.
⏱️
Sweet spot: 3–6 months post-IPO. Lock-up periods expire (~90–180 days), insiders can sell, supply hits the market, and price finds its real floor. That's when opportunity can appear.
🚀
SpaceX note: A once-in-a-generation company with $10B+ real revenue — but hype will almost certainly inflate early pricing. History says: patience wins.
⚠️
This is data, not advice. Every IPO is different. Some beat the odds. Always do your own research and consult a licensed financial professional before investing anything.
